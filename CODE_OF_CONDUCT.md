# Code of conduct

Orange welcomes contributors and users with different backgrounds and levels of experience. This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews and other project-managed spaces.

- Be respectful and address the work rather than attacking the person.
- Give concrete, constructive feedback. Disagreement and candid criticism are welcome; insults, intimidation and personal harassment are not.
- Do not post discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, threats, impersonation or someone else's private information.
- Keep discussions relevant. Do not spam, repeatedly derail a discussion or continue unwanted contact after being asked to stop.
- Respect maintainers' moderation decisions and other people's boundaries.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock discussions, restrict participation or block accounts to protect participants. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact. Project participation does not excuse violations of GitHub's own rules.

For misconduct visible on GitHub, use the platform's **Report content** controls; moderators can also act on conduct they observe in this repository. Do not repeat private information or harassment in a public issue. The project does not currently publish a separate private conduct-reporting address. For sensitive platform abuse, use [GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/).

This is an original, project-specific policy, not a claim of affiliation with a third-party conduct program. Security vulnerabilities follow the separate [security policy](SECURITY.md).
